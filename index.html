Our Story

A Taste of
Two Worlds

Timbo's Takeaway is a family-run kitchen in the heart of Fyshwick — where classic Australian comfort food meets the rich, vibrant flavours of Bhutan.

We believe food is about warmth, generosity, and community. Whether you're grabbing a steak burger on your lunch break or sitting down to a bowl of Ema Datshi, you're part of our family.

Everything is made fresh daily with love and care — just like home.
